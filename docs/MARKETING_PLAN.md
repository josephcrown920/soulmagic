# MARKETING PLAN — Soul

## 1. Positioning
**One-liner:** Soul is the consistency studio for video creators — train a LoRA of your face, lock a color grade, ship on-brand video without juggling five tools.

**Category:** Creator AI / Video post-production
**Wedge:** Identity + grade consistency across every clip (not "another AI video generator")

## 2. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
- **Primary:** Independent creators (5k–200k followers) shipping short-form weekly — coaches, founders, podcasters, course-sellers.
- **Secondary:** Small studios / agencies producing branded content for multiple clients who need preset reusability and quotas.
- **Geo wedge:** Nigeria + diaspora (NGN pricing via Paystack is a real moat vs US-only tools).

## 3. Core Messaging
| Pain | Promise |
|------|---------|
| "I look different in every video" | Locked face identity via your trained LoRA |
| "My grade is inconsistent across clips" | One preset → applied frame-by-frame |
| "I bounce between Topaz, Resolve, ComfyUI, Premiere" | One tab, GPU-powered |
| "AI tools don't accept Naira" | NGN + USD billing built-in |

## 4. Channels (ranked by leverage)
1. **Twitter / X** — before/after pairs, the portrait-set image. High visual ROI.
2. **LinkedIn** — "I made these 4 portraits in 60 seconds" carousels for the founder/coach segment.
3. **YouTube Shorts / TikTok** — 30s screen recordings of the train→generate flow.
4. **Reddit** — r/creators, r/SocialMediaMarketing, r/NigeriaTech.
5. **Partnerships** — 3–5 mid-tier creators trained for free in exchange for one share each.

## 5. 30 / 60 / 90 day plan

### Days 0–30 — Proof
- Ship landing page with the portrait-set hero (✅ done this turn).
- Train 5 founder LoRAs (free) + record their before/after.
- Publish 1 case study + 10 before/after pairs on X.
- **Goal:** 100 sign-ups, 10 paying users.

### Days 31–60 — Loop
- Add referral credit ("invite 3, get 1 month Pro free").
- Launch 4 prompt-template packs (LinkedIn, Course Creator, Podcaster, eCom).
- Start an "On-brand" newsletter — 1 case study/week.
- **Goal:** 500 sign-ups, 60 paying, $2k MRR.

### Days 61–90 — Scale
- Paid: $500/mo X Ads + $500/mo Meta retargeting on visitors of `/`.
- Studio plan outreach to 20 small agencies.
- Affiliate program (30% recurring for 12 months).
- **Goal:** 2,000 sign-ups, 200 paying, $8k MRR.

| Metric | 30d | 60d | 90d |

| Cost per sign-up (paid) | — | $4 | $3 |

## 7. Pricing & offers
- Free: 1 job, watermarked — wedge for virality (watermark = ad).
- Pro: removes watermark + 50 jobs/mo.
- Studio: priority queue + 5 LoRAs.
- **Launch offer:** First 100 paying users get "Founding Creator" lifetime 30% off.

## 8. Copy bank (use anywhere)
- "One LoRA. Every channel. Same face."
- "Ship on-brand video without 5 tools."
- "Your grade. Your face. One drag."
- "Cinema-grade output from a phone clip."

## 9. Risks
- **Replicate cost spike** → cap monthly GPU spend per plan; queue jobs.
- **Consistency complaints** → publish a "good training photo" guide on `/train`.
- **Lookalike copycats** → defend with NGN pricing + creator partnerships.
